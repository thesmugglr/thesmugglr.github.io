Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The Smugglr is a student-run noticeboard where students buy, sell, rent and give away things on campus. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with any college or institute. This page explains what information the site handles and why. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

  • Sign-in information. When you choose "Continue with college Google", Google tells us your name and your institute email address (an @iiitd.ac.in address is required). We do not receive your Google password, your contacts, your files or your calendar.
  • What you add to the site. Your display name and optional batch, listings (title, description, price, photos), saved items, messages and photos you send in chats, offers, lost & found posts and their private replies, the thumbs-up you give after a deal, and any reports or blocks you make.
  • Notifications. Short alerts such as "New message about …" or "… dropped to ₹400" so you can find your conversations and saved items.
  • Phone notifications (only if you turn them on). Your browser gives us a push address and encryption keys for that device. We use them only to send you those alerts, through your browser maker's push service, which sees that an alert was sent but cannot read it. Turning notifications off or signing out attempts to unregister the device; deleting your account removes its saved subscriptions. A network failure may require retrying.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the companies that host the site (see below) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and to check that you are a current student.
  • To run the marketplace: show your listings, deliver your messages, notify you.
  • To approve current students automatically (see below), and to keep it safe: admins review everyone else, plus reports and abuse, and can remove content or suspend accounts.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Automatic approval of students

The college publishes the roll numbers of current B.Tech students on its public website. To approve those students without waiting for an admin, we keep a private list of only the five-digit codes taken from those roll numbers (joining year and serial number). We do not keep names, programs or any other detail from that page. If your institute address ends in one of those codes you are approved automatically; otherwise an admin reviews your request. The codes are restricted to trusted backend administration. Operators need to refresh them each semester; this is not an automatic enrollment-revocation process.

Who can see what

  • Other approved members can see your display name, optional batch and listings. They cannot see your email address.
  • Chats are accessible to their participants and authorized administrators. Administrators' database access is not limited to reported messages. Lost & found replies are accessible to the poster, the person who wrote and authorized administrators.
  • Thumbs-up. Other members see only how many buyers said a seller's deals went smoothly, never who said it.
  • Admins can see members' institute email addresses, the approval list and reports.
  • Photos are stored in private storage and shown through short-lived links to approved members. Photos are resized in your browser before upload, which also removes hidden camera data such as location.
  • Anyone who has not signed in and been approved sees none of this.

Services that handle data for us

  • Google provides sign-in.
  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in sessions and file storage (project region: Mumbai, India).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google when a page loads.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla) delivers phone notifications if you turn them on.

These providers process data under their own privacy terms. We share information with no one else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Listings are hidden from others after 30 days unless the seller renews them, and you can delete your own listings at any time. Account details, messages and reports are kept while your account exists so conversations and moderation records stay intact. You can delete your account yourself at any time from your profile page: that removes your profile, listings and their photos, chats, offers, saved items, requests, bundles, lost & found posts and notification settings. Chat attachment files may remain in private storage after their message rows are deleted; automatic cleanup of those objects is not implemented. A thumbs-up you gave another seller stays in their count, without your name.

Your choices

  • Edit your display name and batch on your profile page, and delete or edit your listings.
  • Block people and report listings or messages.
  • Delete your account from your profile page, or ask for a copy of your data by contacting us.
  • Turn phone notifications on or off for each device on your profile page.
  • Revoke the site's access to your Google sign-in at any time in your Google account's security settings.

Security

The site uses encrypted connections, per-user access rules in the database and private file storage. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not put sensitive personal details (phone numbers, room numbers, ID numbers) in listings or public descriptions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions or requests: contact the operators through the GitHub profile github.com/AbhikRao, or message a site admin.

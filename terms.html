Terms of use

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The Smugglr is a free, student-run noticeboard for students. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with any college or institute. By signing in you agree to these house rules. They are meant to keep the community friendly and safe, not to be a legal contract written by a lawyer.

Who can use it

  • Current students with an @iiitd.ac.in Google account. Current B.Tech students whose institute address matches the institute's public student list are approved automatically; everyone else is approved by an admin before they can browse or post.
  • You are responsible for what happens under your account. Do not share it.

What you may list

  • Things you own and are allowed to sell, rent or give away, described honestly with real photos.
  • Not allowed: anything illegal, stolen, unsafe or counterfeit; weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco or vaping products; exam papers or answers, or anything that helps people cheat; adult content; live animals; anything that breaks IIIT-Delhi rules.
  • No spam, scams, fake listings, fake offers or pressure tactics.

Behaving well

  • Be respectful in chats. No harassment, hate, threats or sharing other people's private details.
  • Do not try to break, overload or get around the site's security or access rules.

Deals are between you and the other person

  • The site only helps people find each other and talk. It does not handle payments, delivery, returns or disputes, and it is not a party to any deal.
  • Check items before you pay, agree prices clearly, and meet in busy campus places such as the library, mess or hostel lobby. Keep your conversation on the site so admins can help if something goes wrong.

Moderation

Admins can remove listings or messages, decline or suspend accounts, and act on reports, with or without notice, to protect the community. Use Report on a listing or message if you see something that breaks these rules, and Block to stop someone contacting you.

No guarantees

The site is provided as it is, without promises that it will always be available or error-free. As a free student project we cannot be responsible for what members do, for the condition or legality of items, or for losses from deals. Use your own judgement.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. You allow the site to store and display it to other members so the marketplace works, and you promise you have the right to post it.

Ending your use

You can stop using the site at any time and ask for your account to be removed (see the privacy policy). We may suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will change the date above when we do. Continuing to use the site means you accept the updated terms. Questions: contact the operators through the GitHub profile github.com/AbhikRao, or message a site admin.
